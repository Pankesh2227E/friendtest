50 “How Well Do You Know Me” Questions to Ask Your Friends

Last updated: October 13, 2026 • Curated for friendship tests & social media quizzes

Looking for the best questions to include in your friendship test? We gathered 50 top-rated questions sorted into Easy Warm-ups, Funny Everyday Habits, and Tricky Deep Cut Secrets. Copy individual questions below or tap the button to pre-fill the quiz creator directly!

⚡ Use These Questions in Your Quiz

Part 1: Easy Warm-Up Questions (1 to 15)

1. What is my favorite food or cheat meal?
2. What month was I born in?
3. Am I a tea lover, coffee addict, or neither?
4. What is my favorite color to wear?
5. Do I prefer dogs, cats, or no pets?
6. What is my go-to fast food joint?
7. Am I an iPhone or Android user?
8. What is my favorite movie or binge-watch show?
9. Do I prefer sweet desserts or spicy savory snacks?
10. What is my favorite season of the year?
11. What is my favorite ice cream flavor?
12. Do I prefer beach vacations or mountain road trips?
13. What pizza topping do I always request?
14. Am I a morning lark or a midnight owl?
15. What music genre do I listen to the most?

Part 2: Funny Everyday Habits (16 to 35)

16. How do I usually text (rapid-fire sentences or a massive novel)?
17. What do I waste the most money on without regret?
18. What is my weirdest pet peeve that instantly irritates me?
19. How long does it realistically take me to get ready to leave the house?
20. What is my phone battery percentage usually sitting at?
21. Do I reply to texts instantly or leave people on read for 12 hours?
22. What was my favorite cartoon show when I was a kid?
23. If I win the lottery tomorrow, what is the very first thing I buy?
24. What is my most frequently used emoji?
25. How do I handle seeing a spider or insect in my room?
26. Would I survive a zombie apocalypse?
27. What is my guiltiest pleasure YouTube or TikTok search?
28. Am I always 10 minutes early or 15 minutes late?
29. What is my absolute worst habit?
30. What food can I eat every day for a year without getting tired of it?
31. What is my signature dance move at parties?
32. How do I act when someone takes photos of me?
33. Which social media app do I scroll on the most?
34. What is the silliest thing I have ever gotten overly competitive about?
35. Can I keep a surprise secret, or do I accidentally spoil it?

Part 3: Tricky Deep-Cut Secrets (36 to 50)

36. What is my biggest irrational fear?
37. What is my dream career or passion project?
38. Who is my celebrity crush?
39. What is the one thing that instantly puts me in a good mood?
40. Have I ever broken a bone or had stitches?
41. What is my favorite childhood memory?
42. What is something I claim to dislike, but secretly enjoy?
43. What is my proudest personal achievement?
44. If I could have one superpower, which would I choose?
45. What kind of birthday present would I appreciate the most?
46. What was the subject of our biggest inside joke?
47. What is my hidden talent that almost nobody knows about?
48. Do I make decisions using logic or my gut emotion?
49. What is my dream car or dream travel destination?
50. Who was my best friend when I was 8 years old?
