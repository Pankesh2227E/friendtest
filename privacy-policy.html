🔒 Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 13, 2026 | Fake Friend Tester

At Fake Friend Tester (the "Service", "we", "us", or "our"), your privacy is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy explains in plain language what minimal information we collect when you use our website, how that data is handled, and how you can delete your information at any time.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the bare minimum data required to power the quiz experience:

  • Quiz Creator Information: When you create a quiz, you provide a nickname or first name, gender preference (used for character avatars), and your answers to 10 trivia questions.
  • Quiz Player Information: When a friend takes your quiz, they input a player nickname and their guesses so your scoreboard can calculate their score.
  • Anonymous Usage Logs: Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, and timestamp) are stored temporarily for security rate limiting and DDoS protection.

2. What We NEVER Collect

We explicitly do not collect, request, or store:

  • Passwords or social media account credentials.
  • Phone numbers, street addresses, or GPS coordinates.
  • Credit card, banking, or payment information.
  • Invasive cross-site advertising identifiers.

3. How Quiz Answers Are Stored

Your quiz questions and player guesses are stored in a secure cloud database (MongoDB Atlas) or local encrypted JSON storage solely to render your scoreboard. Answers are never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Fake Friend Tester does not use invasive advertising cookies. We use standard browser localStorage only to remember your audio mute/unmute preference and save your active quiz creator link so you do not lose it if you refresh the page.

5. Quiz and Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. If you wish to delete a quiz you created or wipe your entry from a friend's leaderboard, simply submit a request on our Contact Us page with your quiz link or ID. We promptly delete the record from our database.

6. Minimum Age and Teen Safety (COPPA Compliance)

Fake Friend Tester is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information, please contact us immediately for immediate removal.

7. Entertainment Only Disclaimer

All quizzes, rankings, and leaderboard statuses (such as "Real One" or "Block!") are generated strictly for comedy, social entertainment, and lighthearted fun. They do not constitute psychological assessments or formal relationship counseling.

8. Contact Our Privacy Team

For any questions or privacy inquiries, contact us via our Contact Us page or by emailing privacy@dontblockme.site.
