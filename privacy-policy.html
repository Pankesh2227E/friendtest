🛡️ Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Don't Block Me

At Don't Block Me (the "Service", "we", "us", or "our"), your privacy is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy outlines what minimal information we collect when you use our website and interactive Fake Friend Test tools, how that information is utilized, and how we protect your personal rights.

✨ Quick Privacy Promise: We never sell your data, we never require passwords or social media logins, and we do not store sensitive personal information like credit cards, phone numbers, or private emails.

1. Information We Collect

We strive to collect only the bare minimum data required to deliver our interactive quiz service:

  • Quiz Creator Information: When you create a quiz, you provide a nickname or first name, gender preference (for customized avatar graphics), and your selected answers to 10 fun trivia questions.
  • Quiz Player Information: When a friend takes your quiz, they input a player name or nickname and submit their answers to calculate a loyalty score.
  • Anonymous Usage Analytics: We may collect standard aggregate server logs (such as request timestamps and browser user-agent) to monitor server health, prevent denial-of-service abuse, and optimize performance.

2. What We NEVER Collect

We explicitly do NOT request or store:

  • Social media passwords or credentials.
  • Phone numbers, street addresses, or physical locations.
  • Financial, payment, or credit card details.
  • Biometric data or sensitive personal identifiers.

3. How We Use the Information

The information submitted is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To generate your unique quiz URL and administrative scoreboard access key.
  • To compare player guesses against the creator's answers and compute accurate scores.
  • To present the quiz creator with their leaderboard showing which friends took the challenge.

4. Data Storage and Security

All quiz data is stored in secure, encrypted databases with strict access limitations. We utilize automated safeguards to prevent malicious attempts, rate-limit brute force submissions, and maintain system integrity.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Don't Block Me uses standard browser LocalStorage and minimal session tokens solely to store your sound effects toggle preference (mute/unmute) and remember your recently created quiz link so you don't lose it if you refresh the browser. We do not use persistent advertising tracking cookies.

6. User Control and Data Deletion

You maintain complete sovereignty over your information. If you wish to delete a quiz you created or remove your name from a scoreboard, you can easily submit a deletion request via our Contact Us page with your quiz ID or link. Deletion requests are processed promptly within 24–48 hours.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our service is designed as family-friendly, general audience social entertainment. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove the data.

8. Policy Revisions

We may occasionally revise this Privacy Policy to reflect feature additions or operational improvements. Any updates will be published directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding your privacy or data practices, please reach out through our official Contact Us page or email us at privacy@dontblockme.site.
