A "how well do you know me" quiz is the most entertaining way to challenge your inner circle, discover which friends remember the details of your life, and expose who is simply pretending. Setting up your personalized test takes less than two minutes and costs nothing.

12 Great Sample Questions to Ask Your Friends

Here are 12 proven questions that strike the right balance between fun facts and close-friend secrets:

  • What is my absolute go-to comfort food when I'm stressed?
  • How do I usually text (rapid-fire short messages vs. one long paragraph)?
  • What was my favorite cartoon or video game growing up?
  • What is my biggest pet peeve that instantly annoys me?
  • If I could hop on a plane tomorrow, where is my dream travel destination?
  • Am I an early-bird morning person or a late-night scroller?
  • What does my ideal Saturday night look like?
  • What is my favorite music genre or hype track?
  • How do I react when unexpected drama happens?
  • What is the one snack I can never say no to?
  • What is my secret talent that few people know about?
  • Who was my childhood role model or hero?

Tips for Crafting Good Questions

The best quizzes mix accessible everyday memories with a couple of tricky curveballs. Avoid questions that are mean-spirited or reveal sensitive private info. If you need more inspiration, check out our curated collection of 50 how well do you know me questions for friends.

What Counts as a Good Score?

Scores run from 0 to 10 points (one point per matched answer):

  • 8 to 10 Points: Verified "Real One"! This person pays close attention to your life and knows your genuine preferences.
  • 4 to 7 Points: Good friend. They know the main facts but may have missed a couple of tricky specifics.
  • 0 to 3 Points: Comical fake friend alert! Take it with a laugh and use it as an excuse to catch up over coffee.
