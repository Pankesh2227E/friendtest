🌟 Behind Don't Block Me

About Us – The Mission Behind the Fake Friend Test

Welcome to Don't Block Me, the viral online destination designed to make friendship reflection fun, engaging, and delightfully revealing. In a world full of superficial social media interactions, double-taps, and endless followers, we set out to build an interactive fake friend test that helps real people celebrate their genuine bonds.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is simple yet powerful: to create playful, transparent digital moments that bring friends closer together. We believe genuine connections are anchored in mutual understanding, shared memories, laughter, and authentic loyalty. By turning friendship trivia into an interactive experience, we give people an engaging way to test who truly pays attention and who is merely pretending.

💡 Why We Created the Fake Friend Test

Everyone has experienced the difference between a ride-or-die best friend and a convenient acquaintance. While true friends stand beside you through life's triumphs and setbacks, a fake friend often disappears when things get tough. We wanted to design a gamified, lighthearted experience where anyone can craft custom questions about their personality, tastes, quirks, and habits—then see who scores at the top of the leaderboard!

1. Gamified Friendship Trivia 🎮 Create 10 customized questions that reveal your true personality. Share the link instantly with your friends and group chats.
2. Live Honesty Leaderboard 📊 Track your friends' scores in real time. Spot the loyal confidants and laugh together with your inner circle.
3. Zero Harm, Pure Fun 💛 Our quizzes are crafted for comedy, social connection, and playful rivalry. We believe in building bonds, not breaking hearts.
4. Privacy First Always 🔒 We never collect sensitive personal information, phone numbers, or passwords. Your trust and privacy are our top priorities.

🌱 Our Core Values

Authenticity: We value sincerity over pretense. Our quizzes highlight what makes real friendships tick.

Positivity & Humor: Even when calling out fake friends, our platform is rooted in wholesome humor, cute animations, and playful sound effects.

Accessibility: No complicated app installations or account signups required. Anyone with a web browser can participate in seconds.
