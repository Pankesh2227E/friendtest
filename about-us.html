🌟 Behind Fake Friend Tester

About Us – The Mission Behind Fake Friend Tester

Welcome to Fake Friend Tester, the online destination designed to make friendship reflection fun, engaging, and delightfully revealing. In a world full of superficial social media interactions, double-taps, and endless followers, we set out to build an interactive quiz experience that helps real people celebrate their genuine bonds.

🎯 Who Runs the Site and Why

Fake Friend Tester is built and maintained by an independent product and engineering team passionate about building lighthearted, positive web experiences. We created this platform to give friends a fun excuse to laugh, reconnect, and playfully compete on a real-time leaderboard.

💡 Why We Created the Fake Friend Test

Everyone has experienced the difference between a ride-or-die best friend and a convenient acquaintance. While true friends stand beside you through life's triumphs and setbacks, an insincere acquaintance often disappears when things get tough. We wanted to design a gamified, lighthearted experience where anyone can craft custom questions about their personality, tastes, quirks, and habits—then see who scores at the top of the leaderboard!

1. Gamified Friendship Trivia 🎮 Create 10 customized questions that reveal your true personality. Share the link instantly with your friends and group chats.
2. Live Honesty Leaderboard 📊 Track your friends' scores in real time. Spot the loyal confidants and laugh together with your inner circle.
3. Zero Harm, Pure Fun 💛 Our quizzes are crafted for comedy, social connection, and playful rivalry. We believe in building bonds, not breaking hearts.
4. Privacy First Always 🔒 We never collect sensitive personal information, phone numbers, or passwords. Your trust and privacy are our top priorities.

🌱 Our Core Values

Authenticity: We value sincerity over pretense. Our quizzes highlight what makes real friendships tick.

Positivity & Humor: Even when calling out fake friends, our platform is rooted in wholesome humor, cute animations, and playful sound effects.

Accessibility: No complicated app installations or account signups required. Anyone with a web browser can participate in seconds.
